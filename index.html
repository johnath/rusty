feeling rusty?

It actually doesn't matter at all why we're rusty or whether you think we have a right to be. What matters is that a lot of us are pretty out of practice when it comes to the human connection parts of work. Humans are social critters. We can do creative, expansive, increidble work. But we're worse at it, across a wide set of measures, when we try to go it alone. If you're feeling a bit rusty, you're in good company. This project is a much-needed vinegar soak for connection at work.
